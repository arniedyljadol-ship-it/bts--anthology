Identity & Philosophy

Group Background

🛡️

Bulletproof Origins

Formed under Big Hit Entertainment by Bang Si-hyuk, Bangtan Sonyeondan translates to "Bulletproof Boy Scouts." The name signifies shielding youth against the stereotypes, judgments, and societal pressures aimed at them like bullets. In 2017, they introduced the extended name "Beyond The Scene".

✍️

Self-Produced Artistry

Unlike many traditional acts, all seven members actively compose, produce, and write their own music. Their discography tackles mental health, youth alienation, unfair societal structures, self-acceptance, and personal growth.

🕊️

UNICEF & "Speak Yourself"

In collaboration with UNICEF, BTS launched the global #LoveMyself campaign against youth violence. In 2018, group leader RM addressed the United Nations General Assembly, delivering an iconic message: "Find your name and find your voice by speaking yourself."

Eras & Milestones

Chronological History

2013 — 2014

Debut & The School Trilogy

Debuted on June 13, 2013, with single album 2 Cool 4 Skool and lead single "No More Dream". Their early hip-hop releases confronted heavy academic pressure and conformist expectations placed on young Koreans.

2015 — 2016

The Most Beautiful Moment in Life & Wings

Earned their first music program trophy with "I Need U" from HYYH Pt.1. In 2016, Wings and title track "Blood Sweat & Tears" became an international breakthrough, peaking at #26 on the US Billboard 200.

2017 — 2019

Love Yourself & Global Phenomenon

Won Top Social Artist at the Billboard Music Awards. Love Yourself: Tear (2018) made history as the first Korean album to top the Billboard 200, leading into a sold-out worldwide stadium tour.

2020 — 2021

Billboard Hot 100 Dominance

Released "Dynamite", becoming the first all-South Korean act to hit #1 on the US Billboard Hot 100. Followed with consecutive chart-toppers "Life Goes On" and "Butter", as well as multiple Grammy nominations.

2022 & Beyond

Anthology 'Proof' & Solo Chapter

Celebrated 9 years together with the anthology album Proof. Members launched critically acclaimed solo records, world tours, and completed South Korean military service ahead of their group return.

Historic Chart Records

The first Korean act to top both the Billboard Hot 100 and Artist 100 charts simultaneously. They also became the first band since The Beatles to score three #1 albums on the Billboard 200 in less than 12 months.

Wembley & Stadium Tours

The first Asian act in history to headline and completely sell out London's iconic Wembley Stadium, the Rose Bowl in California, and Citi Field in New York.

Order of Cultural Merit

Recognized as the youngest-ever recipients of South Korea's Order of Cultural Merit for their monumental role in elevating Korean culture, art, and language internationally.
